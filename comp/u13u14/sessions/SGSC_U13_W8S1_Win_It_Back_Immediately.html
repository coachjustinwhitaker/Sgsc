Philosophy Connection

Adapted from a Modern Soccer Coach session on counterpressing. Our reaction the instant we lose the ball has been slow, and that's the gap today closes. The games are number-neutral by design, so no reshaping for our 4-2-3-1, just brought them onto our field as designed. We're only coaching the reaction after we lose it today, not the decision of when to hold back.

Session Objectives
  • React immediately to loss of possession with pressure on the ball and compact support around the duel
  • Recover the ball in the same area it was lost, then transition with purpose rather than resetting
  • Show up as a coordinated pressing unit, first presser and covering support, not isolated sprints at the ball
  • Carry the habit from the isolated pressing games into the full scrimmage at the end

Activity 2 of 4  ·  Small-Sided Game
8v5 Warm-Up Counterpress
30×30 Yard Grid  ·  Four Quadrants  ·  20 minutes
Possession Group (us)
8 players
Pressing Group
5 players
Grid
30×30 yd, 4 quadrants
Duration
20 min
30×30 Grid — Four Quadrants, Mini-Goals on the Perimeter
Objective Get everyone's engine running while immediately demanding today's habit: react the instant the ball is lost. We are coaching the possession group here. Their job is to keep the ball, but the moment it's gone, that's the read we care about, not the group trying to win it.

Possession Group (8, us)

Keep the ball inside the grid. Score a point every time the ball circulates through all four quadrants in a single sequence. When it's lost, counterpress immediately.

Pressing Group (5)

Win possession as quickly as possible, then score in any of the mini-goals set outside the grid.

Coaching Points
  • The instant you lose it, who closes first, and who covers?
  • First player pressures the ball, is the second already covering the pass?
  • Stay compact as a unit. Are you reacting together or chasing individually?

40×30 Zone — Attacking Group (Blue) vs Defending Group + GK (Red)
Objective Bring the same read into a more realistic final-third picture, with a real goal and a real goalkeeper on our side. We are coaching the attacking group here, the ones with the numerical advantage. Their job when the ball is lost is to counterpress immediately as a group, not scatter forward and hope.

Attacking Group (6, us)

Organize with a numerical advantage and attack the central full-size goal. When possession is lost, counterpress aggressively as a group.

Defending Group (5+GK)

Win the ball cleanly and transition into any of the mini-goals set around the outside of the zone.

Coaching Points
  • When you lose it, is the whole group reacting immediately, or just the player closest to the ball?
  • Support around the duel, who's covering the presser's back?
Activity 4 of 4  ·  Tactical Game
8v8 “Lock Them In”
60×40 Yard Zone  ·  Three Channels  ·  25 minutes
Format
8v8
Field
60×40 yd, 3 channels
Scoring
Goal = 2, same-sector recovery = 1
Duration
25 min
60×40 Zone — Three Channels, Full-Size Goals Both Ends
Objective Put everything from today into a live game. Regular match rules, full-sized goals, with one added reward: recovering the ball in the same sector it was lost is worth a point, on top of the two points a goal is worth. The channels make the sector visible so players can actually see and feel where a counterpress happened.

Rule

Standard 8v8, full-sized goals both ends. Field is divided into three vertical channels and split by a halfway line, giving six total sectors. If a team loses the ball and wins it back in the same sector, that's 1 point. A goal is worth 2.

Coaching Points
  • Which sector did you lose the ball in? Is the group reacting there, specifically, or resetting?
  • Same read from Parts 2 and 3: compact, immediate, together
  • Celebrate a clean recovery out loud, that is today's habit showing up live
