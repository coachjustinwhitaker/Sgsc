Step to the Ball

Players move to meet the pass and decide: receive early, take a touch, or let it run. Three stations, then a scrimmage.

90 minWhite and Blue, about 30 playersGrass · B5 Me & the MomentA coach at every station
At a glanceWarm-up1 Finish2 Deliver3 Receive4 Play
The session
  • –Warm-up10′
  • 1Finish. 4v2 to Goal15′
  • 2Deliver. 4v4 Back to Back Goals15′
  • 3Receive. 3v3 With End Zones15′
  • 4Play. Final Scrimmage35′
The one idea: step to the ball, then decide. Light pass: keep stepping in and take it early. Hard pass: take a touch. Pass gets by you: let it run and go with it. Passers weight the ball so the receiver can step to it.

How the stations run

  • Six groups of 5. Three groups wear one color and three groups wear a second color. Each station has one group of each color, so every station is a 5v5 between the two colors.
  • Whistle every 15 minutes: 14 to play and 1 to move. One color rotates 1, then 2, then 3. The other color rotates 3, then 2, then 1, so groups face different opponents each round.
  • A coach at every station. The coach with the balls feeds and restarts play.

Equipment

Balls, a pile at each stationCones, lots (all three stations)4 goals (2 facing each other at Station 1, 2 back to back at Station 2)Pinnies, 2 colors
Key terms
Step to the ball
Move toward the pass instead of waiting for it to arrive.
Weight
How hard a pass is hit. A light pass is slow and a hard pass is fast.
Neutral
A player who plays for whichever team has the ball.
End zone (Station 3)
The short zone at each end of the field. Each one has a 1v1.

Finish: 4v2 to Goal

Teams of 5 with keepers · midline
15′
Control the ball until there is a chance to create a finish. Then pass it into space and finish immediately.
10 playersGrass2 goals facing each other

Setup

  • Two goals facing each other, about 24 yards apart. Each team has its own keeper.
  • Cones across the middle, halfway between the goals, mark the midline. Wide cones mark the sides, about 36 yards wide.
  • Each team is 5: 4 field players and a keeper.
  • To start, one team attacks with 4 against 2 defenders. The defending team's other 2 players wait wide.

Rules

  • 4 attackers against 2 defenders. Keepers stay in their own goal.
  • Free touches until a pass takes the ball over the midline.
  • After the ball crosses the midline you get two touches at most. A third touch gives the ball to the defending keeper.
  • After about 7 minutes the coach switches it to one touch past the midline. A second touch gives the ball to the defending keeper.
  • If the defense wins the ball, the ball goes out, or a goal is scored, the attackers drop to 2 defenders. The other team adds its 2 waiting players and attacks the other goal.
  • The two attackers nearest the goal they now defend stay as defenders. The other two go wide and wait.

Scoring

  • A goal is one point.

Run it

  1. Two touches (about 7′). Play with the two-touch rule past the midline.
  2. One touch (about 7′). Switch to one touch past the midline. Swap keepers.
Coach it: cue, then ask
  • Move to space and expect the ball. Do not wait on your heels.What did you see before you moved?
  • Weight the pass so your teammate can step to it.Could your teammate step into that pass? What would have made it easier?
  • In the one-touch zone, decide before it arrives.What was your plan for the ball before it got to you?

Deliver: 4v4 Back to Back Goals

Teams of 5 with keepers · coach joins as a neutral
15′
This game works four actions: delivering a cross or cutback with the weight and pace to reach a moving target, recognizing the moment to deliver early or late, setting the body to finish a moving ball, and resetting right after a miss.
10 playersGrass2 goals back to backNo touch limit

Setup

  • Two goals back to back in the center, mouths facing opposite ways. A keeper stands in front of each.
  • Corner cones mark an area about 36 by 37 yards. Players will not use all of it, so shrink it if it plays cramped.
  • Two cones, one at the top and one at the bottom of the area, line up with the goals. They mark the split line, the line through the backs of the goals.
  • 4v4 plus a keeper each.

Rules

  • No touch limit.
  • You can drive the ball across the split line into your attacking side.
  • The player who drives it across cannot score. They have to cross it or cut it back to a teammate to finish.
  • The coach can jump in as a neutral who plays for the team with the ball. Passing only, no shooting.

Scoring

  • One point per goal.

Run it

  1. Play. The coach plays a ball in. After a goal or the ball going out, the coach plays in a new ball.
  2. Coach as neutral. Jump in whenever you want to add a passing option.
Coach it: cue, then ask
  • Move to the ball when it is coming across.What did you see that told you to move?
  • Put the ball where your teammate can meet it.Where did you want the cross to land, and did it?
  • Cross it or cut it back to the center.Who was free in the center when you delivered?
  • Missed it? Reset right away.What did you do straight after the miss?
3

Receive: 3v3 With End Zones

Teams of 5 · non-stop
15′
Move the ball through the center and get it received in the far end zone. The end zone player has a defender on them, so they step to the ball and decide.
10 playersGrass3v3 in the center1v1 in each end zone

Setup

  • A field about 30 yards wide. Each end zone is about 7 yards deep and the center is about 28 yards. Cones mark the zones.
  • Each team has 3 players in the center and 1 in each end zone, so every end zone is a 1v1.

Rules

  • A ball starts in one end zone.
  • The ball has to go through the center. It must touch a center teammate between end zones, so no direct end zone to end zone pass.
  • After a score, move the ball right back the other way. Non-stop.
  • If the defense wins the ball, the game flips and they attack the other way.
  • End zone players stay in their zone.

Scoring

  • A score is the ball received under control in the far end zone.
  • The first score is 1 point. Each score in a row doubles: 1, then 2, then 4. A score is never worth more than 4.
  • The count starts over any time the ball goes out or is turned over.

Run it

  1. Play. When the ball goes out, the coach feeds a new one.
  2. Rotate. Every 3 to 4 minutes, rotate the end zone players and the center players.
Coach it: cue, then ask
  • End zone player, move to the ball before the defender does.When did you decide to step in?
  • Center players, weight the pass for the person receiving it.Could your teammate step to that pass?
  • After a score, move the ball right back the other way.What did you see before you played it back?
4

Play: Final Scrimmage

Free play
35′
Free play to see whether stepping to the ball and the decisions carry into a game.
Both squadsGrassOne live set-piece rep

Setup

  • White vs Blue.
  • Set the field and numbers with the coaches on the day.

Rules

  • Free play. No restrictions.

Scoring

  • One point per goal. No extra scoring rules.

Run it

  1. Play. Run the scrimmage for 35 minutes.
  2. Set-piece rep. At some point, call one live set-piece rep.
Coach it: cue, then ask
  • Step to the ball. Do not wait on your heels.When did you move to the ball, and when did you wait?
  • Read the pass before you receive.What did the weight of the pass tell you?
  • Weight the pass for the person receiving it.Could they step into it?
