Passing Mastery Squares — V1 Back Foot / V2 3rd Man / V3 1-2 Combo / V4 Diagonal
Objective Pure technical reps before anything else today, since we're on turf and want volume. Groups of 5 throughout, the four-player variations just rotate the fifth player in as a periodic swap so nobody regroups between patterns.
Four Variations, Corners Numbered 1–4 (Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left)
V1 · Back Foot (~2′)Ball circulates corner to corner around the square. Receive on the back foot, open the body before playing it on.
V2 · 3rd Man (~3′)1→2, 2 lays it back but only to the midpoint between 1 and 2, where 1's player meets it and redirects on to 3, not back to 2. Same pattern continues, 3→2 laid to midpoint→4, then 4→3 laid to midpoint→1. The give-and-receive never repeats between the same two players.
V3 · 1-2 Combo (~3′)A real give-and-go on each edge: 1→2, 2 lays back to midway, 1 plays it right back out to midway again, advance to the next edge using that corner as the new start. Runs 1↔2, then 2↔3, then 3↔4, then 4↔1 to close the loop.
V4 · Diagonal (~2′)Ball goes to the adjacent corner first, then diagonally across to the far corner, crossing the opposite diagonal in the middle. Tests disguised passing and run timing so paths don't collide.
Coaching Points
  • Inside-foot contact, plant foot pointed at the target
  • Weight the pass to the receiver's feet, not their chest
  • Quick set touch to open the next passing angle early
  • Call for it before the ball arrives, don't receive in silence

2v2 First-Touch Transition Game
Gate Entry Into Live Transition to Small Goals  ·  20 minutes
Format
2v2, enter from both ends
Goals
4 Puggs, 2 per side
Duration
20 min
2v2 Box — Enter From Both Ends, Red Attacks Up, Blue Attacks Down
Objective Combine first-touch precision under a constraint with a live 2v2 duel, directly serving the quick-touch and small-numbers work we need more of. One Red and one Blue enter together at each end of the box, so every rep starts as a live, numbers-even 2v2 immediately.

Setup

Square box, two Puggs on the top edge, two on the bottom. At each end there's a central gate, the gap between the two middle cones, and a side gap, between the corner cone and the far Pugg. Red attacks upward into the top Puggs, Blue attacks downward into the bottom Puggs. One Red and one Blue enter together from the top, another Red and Blue pair enter together from the bottom.

Sequence

The ball is served to whichever Red player is receiving that rep. Red's first touch has to go through the central gate, that's the restriction. Blue enters at the same moment through the side gap, no ball, straight into the contest. Since each team already has a player waiting near the goal they're attacking, the entering player's first read is often to find that teammate rather than solve it alone.

Coach Note

One ball per rep, served to Red, since that's the only touch restriction that applies. Alternate which end serves rep to rep so both Red players get the gate work.

Coaching Points
  • Red: first touch through the gate has to be on purpose, not just any touch
  • Blue: get through your gap early, arriving late means defending from behind
  • You already have a teammate near your goal, look for them before trying to solve it alone
  • Whoever wins the entry duel dictates who's attacking and who's recovering

2 Lines vs GK — Passing Combination Into a Finish
Objective Build the habit of finishing off a combination, not just shooting in isolation. This is the direct response to Saturday, we scored six and still didn't convert cleanly in the box.

Setup

20x15 yard grid anchored at the corner of the box. Two servers and two target players positioned as shown in the diagram, GK in goal.

Pattern

Servers combine with a tight give-and-go, then the ball works diagonally through both target players before the final pass sets up the finish. Follow the numbered sequence exactly as drawn.

Coach Note

Walk the passing pattern through slowly first, then add the finish once it's clean. Rotate the lines every couple reps so everyone touches every role.

Coaching Points
  • First-time finish when the service allows it, no unnecessary extra touch in the box
  • The weight of the third pass has to invite the run, not just reach it
  • GK: work your angles, this is live shooting even though it's semi-structured
  • Quality of the combination decides the quality of the chance, that's the whole point today

Finishing Circuit — Three Service Points, Mannequin at Each Finish
Objective High-volume finishing reps from three different angles, each with a mannequin to negotiate before the shot. Direct answer to Saturday, more touches in the box, more looks on goal, more traffic to deal with.

Setup

Three servers, 1, 4, and 5, feed three finishers, 2, 3, and 6, inside the box. Each finisher has a mannequin between them and goal. 1 serves straight in to 2. 5 serves diagonally across to 3. 4 serves diagonally across to 6.

Sequence

All three stations run at once, ball moving continuously. For 3 and 6, start tight on the mannequin, check away first to sell the separation, then check back into the space that opens up to receive and finish. For 2, the mannequin sits at their back when the ball arrives, receive, turn away from it, then shoot. Rotate servers and finishers regularly so everyone gets every angle and both kinds of movement.

Coaching Points
  • 3 and 6: tight to start, check away to create the separation, then check back to receive
  • 2: first touch has to turn you away from the mannequin, not just control it
  • Different angle, different finish, don't default to the same shot every time
  • Keep the ball moving, the next rep starts the moment this one ends
  • GK: full volume today, work every angle live
