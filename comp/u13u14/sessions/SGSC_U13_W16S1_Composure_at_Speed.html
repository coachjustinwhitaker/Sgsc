Philosophy Connection

B1 Application is individual technique at full U13 game speed against committed, closing defenders. This week the boys get a lot of touches in small-sided games and a lot of B1 actions under real pressure: scan while the defender closes, make the first touch a decision, shield or release, and drive with the head up. Composure is not slowing down; it is seeing the picture early enough that speed does not rush the decision.

Session Objectives
  • Scan while the defender closes and recognise disguised pressure before the ball arrives
  • Make the first touch a decision against a committed defender, opening the next target
  • Shield or release in real time against a fast-closing defender, body between ball and opponent
  • Drive at game speed with the head up, and play the ball back in on the move
  • Take lots of B1 actions in small-sided games at full U13 game speed

Medium Grid — 4 Mini Goals, Square
ObjectiveMake the decision before the ball arrives. Which goal is on, which is closed, and does my first touch open the next target? Individual perception and first-touch decisions at game speed against committed defenders.

Scoring & Rules

Four mini goals form a square. The two end goals are the attack/defend targets: each team attacks one and defends the other, worth 3 points. The two side goals are neutral, either team can score on them, worth 1 point.

Progression

No touch limit to start. When the group is comfortable, bring in a 2-touch maximum. Coach reads the group; no set time.

Coaching Points
  • What did you see before the ball arrived?
  • Which goal did you pick, and why?
  • Did your first touch open the next target?

Smallest Grid — Thirds Marked by Flags, 2 Goals, 5 Circles
ObjectiveLearn the shielding body shape against a live, pressing defender, then decide in the moment: shield it or release it.

Scoring & Rules

Goal alone: 1 point. Ball on or in a circle, shielded away from a defender for 3 seconds: 1 point each time, accumulating with no cap. Shield, then goal on the same possession: 3 points total. Shots only from inside the final thirds, no midfield shooting. Coach counts out loud; the count resets if the ball leaves the circle or is lost.

Thirds, Defenders & Delivery

Flags split the field into thirds. Defenders press live, right up to the circle. No requirements on how the ball reaches the shielder; the action is the shield, not the delivery.

Coaching Points
  • Was your body between the ball and the defender?
  • Shield or release, and what told you?
  • Feel the defender with your arm and back, not your eyes; keep the head up

Biggest Grid — Central Game + Two Wide Lane Channels (Green)
ObjectiveDrive the ball at speed with the head up and decide where it goes. The driver decides; nobody stops, nobody sets up a cross.

Scoring & Rules

Goal: 1 point. A goal off a lane service: 2 points. One neutral player per lane (neutral pinnie), playing for whoever passes them the ball and unopposed only inside the lane. In the lane the player must drive and play the ball back in on the move. A stop or a set-up cross is a foul and the ball goes to the other team.

Restarts & Rotation

As soon as the ball is back in the game, defenders can win it. Lane players rotate with the central game every 2 minutes.

Coaching Points
  • Head up: what did you see while you were driving?
  • Where did you play it, and why?
  • No stopping to set up the cross; drive and deliver in one movement
