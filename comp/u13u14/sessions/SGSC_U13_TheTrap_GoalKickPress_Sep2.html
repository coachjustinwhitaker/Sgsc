Philosophy Connection

C License session 3.3. The problem: their CBs spread wide and deep on a goal kick, and we've been letting them play out clean through a disorganized press. Today installs a coordinated trap, everyone moving to an assigned position off one trigger, not a scramble toward the ball. Improve our ability to recognize the opponent's build-out shape and execute a coordinated press to win the ball or force a mistake in their own half.

Session Objectives
  • Recognize the trigger, their CBs spread wide and deep before the goal kick, and call the press as a team
  • 9 arcs the sprint onto the receiving CB, cutting off the pass back to the GK, applying immediate pressure
  • 11, 10, 6, and 3 lock off every passing lane so there's no easy outlet once 9 presses
  • Move as a collective unit, not just the player closest to the ball
  • Force a mistake or a long ball, then finish the moment if we win it

The Problem — What We're Seeing

The opponent sets up with CBs spread wide and deep in the box and plays out successfully through our disorganized attacking press.

The Fix — Desired Behavior

Build pressure on the ball as a collective unit, and take countermeasures to anticipate and counter, everyone moves to their assigned position off one trigger.

Objective Execute the arc sprint press and cut off the wide outlet. Isolate the 9's specific job, the arcing run that cuts off the pass back to the GK, before adding the rest of the shape.

Setup

Ball always starts with GK 1. Rep ends when either team scores or the ball goes out. Players rotate through the 9/11 positions each rep.

Scoring

We score by winning the ball and shooting into the goal. They score by passing into either pugg target before the press arrives.

Coaching Points
  • CP2 — 9: When the GK passes to the 4, arc the sprint onto the 4, angle the run to cut off the pass back to the GK, apply immediate pressure
  • CP3 — 11: When the 9 presses into the box, move to cut off the passing lane to their 2, prevent the easy wide outlet

Objective Add the 6 and the 3 to the shape. Now the press has cover on both the central lane and the wide 7, not just the initial trigger from the 9.

Setup

Ball always starts with red 1. Rep ends when either team scores or the ball goes out. Two groups, one rotates in each rep.

Scoring

We score by winning the ball and shooting into the goal. They score: 2 by controlling the ball into the endzone, 7 by passing into the central pugg (9), 1 by passing into the right pugg (3). Their 4 cannot score.

Coaching Points
  • CP5 — 6: If our 11 moves outside, sprint to cover the passing lane to their 7, block the half space outlet
  • CP6 — 3: If the 6 steps in to cover the half space lane, get ball-side and tight on their 7, cut off the pass and be ready to intercept

Objective Full team, full shape. Recognize the trigger, call and confirm the press, execute the full coordinated press shape, not just react to the ball being played.

Setup

Us: 9, 11, 6, 3, 10, 7, 8, 2 vs Them: 1, 4, 5, 2, 7, 3, 6, 11. Ball always starts with their 1, GK may choose to build out or play a long ball. No subs available.

Scoring

We score by winning the ball and shooting into the goal. They score by advancing the ball into the endzone at the halfway line.

Coaching Points
  • CP1 — All: When their 4/5 spread wide and deep in the box, call and confirm the press, everyone moves to assigned positions
  • CP4 — 10: When the 9 presses into the box, sprint to the top of the box to cover the central lane, hold position, don't press forward
  • CP7 — 7: When the 9 sprints onto the 4, make a run into the back of the box, ready to cut off the passing lane and finish if we win it
