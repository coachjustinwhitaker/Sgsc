Monthly Governance

How each Board meeting runs, before, during and after
All documents · Annual calendar · Governance · Operations

How to use it: run this list for every regular Board meeting, then clear the checkmarks for the next one. The Board governs through policy, budget and recorded votes (Board manual 2.6). Day-to-day work runs through the operations cycle: a weekly call and a monthly operations report that comes in here. Items without a reference are working practice, not policy.
