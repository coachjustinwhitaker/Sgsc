Annual Calendar

What the club does every year, month by month, and who owns it
All documents · Annual calendar · Governance · Operations

How to use it: what happens at every Board meeting is on the monthly governance page, and the weekly and monthly operations cycle is on the operations page. the calendar runs June to May, the club's planning year: it starts after tryouts, when fall planning goes into action. The seasonal year still runs August 1 to July 31. Competitive teams play it as one season in two halves, so competitive coaches carry over from fall to spring. Filing dates assume the fiscal year ends July 31; if it doesn't, the Treasurer moves them. References point to the manuals: B is the Board Policy Manual and C is the Coaching Policy Manual. Checkmarks save in your own browser only, so they're a personal tracker, not a shared one.
