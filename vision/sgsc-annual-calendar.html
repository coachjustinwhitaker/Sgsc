The calendar runs June to May, the club's planning year. It starts after tryouts, when fall planning goes into action. The seasonal year still runs August 1 to July 31, and competitive teams play it as one season in two halves, so competitive coaches carry over from fall to spring.

What happens at every Board meeting is on the Monthly Governance page. The weekly and monthly operations cycle is on the Operations page.

Filing dates assume the fiscal year ends July 31. References point to the manuals: B is the Board Policy Manual and C is the Coaching Policy Manual. Checkmarks save in your own browser only, so they are a personal tracker, not a shared one.
