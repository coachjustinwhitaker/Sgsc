Field SOP
Updated September 2026
01
Access Info
Lockbox

On the fence closest to the baseball fields, behind the soccer goals.

Combo shared with coaches
Keychain

Small key opens the soccer shed and gates. Key labeled "RR" opens the restrooms. Key labeled "AB" opens the announcer's booth for the lights.

Goals

Secured with a separate combo lock, not a key.

Combo shared with coaches
02
First Coach: Setup

Arrive at least 70 minutes before the first game. Visiting teams often travel over an hour and can start arriving about an hour before kickoff, so bathrooms need to be open before anyone shows up.

1

Line up volunteers

Get a couple of adult volunteers ahead of time to help move goals. Setup takes at least 10-15 minutes.

Goals cannot be moved solo
2

Open the lockbox

Enter the lockbox combo. Take the keychain.

3

Bathrooms first

Unlock the RR padlock on one restroom door and swing the gate open. The padlock won't release the key until it's back in the locked position, so lock it onto the wall to hold the gate open, then pull the key out. Repeat for the second restroom door.

4

Open the shed

Unlock the soccer shed with the small key. It's the light-colored standalone shed running perpendicular to the fence, separate from the blue sheds near the hill. Take only the corner flags. Do not remove any other school equipment. Relock the shed.

5

Unlock the goals

Use the goal combo lock.

6

Move the goals

Use the handles only, with your volunteers helping. The frames are old and re-welded, with weak points at the corners.

Goals cannot be moved solo
7

Set out corner flags

8

Return the keychain

Back to the lockbox, spin the combo. Do not carry keys in your coach's bag.

03
Last Coach: Cleanup

Only do this after the final game or session of the day.

1

Get the keychain

From the lockbox.

2

Move goals back into position

Use the handles, with multiple people helping.

Goals cannot be moved solo
3

Lock the goals

Use the goal combo lock.

4

Lock the restrooms

Both sides, with the RR key. Push the locking arm firmly into the locked position. The key will not release in the open position.

5

Return corner flags

Collect them and return them to the shed. Lock the shed with the small key.

6

Clear the bench areas

Pick up all trash before you go.

7

Return the keychain

Back to the lockbox, spin the combo. Do not take keys home.

04
Lights (Night Games and Later Practices)
1

Get the AB key

From the lockbox.

2

Unlock the announcer's booth

Unlock the door and go in.

3

Find the switches

On the wall straight ahead, near the windows, at about shoulder height on the left, are two switches. These turn on the field lights.

4

Give them time to warm up

Lights take about 10 minutes to reach full brightness. Turn them on with enough lead time before kickoff.

05
Reminders
  • Restroom locks are stubborn. The key won't release unless the locking arm is firmly pushed into the locked position first. Takes some getting used to.
  • Goals use a combo lock, not a key.
  • The small key does double duty. It also opens the main stadium gates near the concrete stairs.
  • Lights take about 10 minutes to warm up. Don't wait until kickoff to flip them on.
  • Keychain stays in the lockbox. Return it after every use, even mid-day. Never in a coach's bag.
  • Line up your goal volunteers before arrival. Not after you get to the field.
