How operations work

  • The Board sets policy and budget. Operations roles carry them out (Board manual 1.2, 2.6).
  • Lead staff runs operations day to day. Until the club hires a lead staff member (Board manual 5.4), the Director of Coaching fills this role.
  • Each role owns its work and decides within policy and budget.
  • Problems surface on the weekly call. Most get solved there. Big ones go to the Board.
  • The monthly operations report sums up the weekly calls for the Board.

Weekly operations call

Early each week, after the weekend's games. Phone or video, 30 minutes. Lead staff runs it. Age Group Coordinators attend. Team managers are welcome to call in.

What gets elevated to the Board

Right away, not at the next call: any abuse or safety concern goes straight to law enforcement or CPS and the safety coordinator (Board manual 8.1). Lead staff tells the President within 24 hours.

To the President within 48 hours, for the Board:

  • A matter that could lead to discipline or a PAD case (7.1).
  • A league fine, sanction or forfeit.
  • A coach who may need a support plan or removal (Coaching manual 1.3).
  • Spending outside the budget, or a contract or agreement (4.11, 2.4).
  • A request for an exception to any policy (1.3).
  • A legal, insurance or media issue.
  • The same problem coming up three weeks running.

Everything else is handled by the role that owns it and shows up in the operations report.
